How Much Do You Know About Cats?

Cats are curious animals with many interesting abilities and behaviors. Here are some fun facts about our feline friends!

1. Cats Sleep a Lot

Cats often sleep for around 12 to 16 hours a day. Kittens and older cats may sleep even more. Sleeping helps them rest and recover energy.

2. Cats Have Amazing Hearing

Cats can hear higher-pitched sounds than humans. Their sensitive hearing helps them notice small animals and other sounds around them.

3. Whiskers Help Cats

A cat's whiskers are very sensitive. They help cats sense nearby objects and changes in their surroundings. Whiskers should never be cut.

4. Cats Communicate in Different Ways

Cats communicate through meowing, purring, body language and tail movements. They may meow to get a human's attention.

5. Every Cat Has Its Own Personality

Some cats are playful and social, while others prefer quiet places and more personal space. Cats can have very different personalities, even if they belong to the same breed.

6. Cats Have Excellent Night Vision

Cats can see better than humans in dim light. However, they still need some light to see; they cannot see in complete darkness.
