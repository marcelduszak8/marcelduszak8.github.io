How to Take Care of a Cat

Cats need food, water, attention and a safe place to live. Taking good care of a cat helps it stay healthy and happy.

Food and Water

Cats need food that is made especially for them. They should always have access to fresh, clean water. Their food should match their age and needs.

Grooming

Cats clean themselves, but some cats need help brushing their fur. Long-haired cats usually need more brushing to prevent their fur from getting tangled.

Playtime and Exercise

Cats enjoy playing with toys, chasing objects and exploring their surroundings. Playing with your cat helps keep it active and provides mental stimulation.

A Clean and Safe Home

Cats need a clean litter box, a comfortable place to sleep and somewhere to hide when they want peace. Keep dangerous objects and toxic plants away from them.

Health

Cats need regular veterinary check-ups and appropriate vaccinations. If your cat acts differently or seems unwell, contact a veterinarian.
